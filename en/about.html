About Us

💔 Our Story

Breakups hurt. And in the middle of that pain, the most common question people ask themselves is: "Will my ex ever come back?"

We built Will My Ex Come Back Calculator to be a friendly, entertaining tool that gives you an honest perspective — blending light-hearted humor with real psychological concepts like attachment theory and the no-contact rule.

Our goal isn't to give you false hope or feed your anxiety. It's to help you pause, reflect, and smile — even just a little — while you're going through one of the hardest emotional experiences of your life.

🎯 What We Offer

  • Free Psychology-Based Calculator — Answer 7 questions based on real relationship patterns.
  • Ghibli-Style Story Video — Get a personalized animated story based on your answers.
  • 100% Private — Your names and details never leave your device.
  • No Signup Required — Just enter names and get instant results.

🔒 Privacy First

Unlike many tools online, we don't sell your data or track your personal information. Everything you enter into the calculator is processed locally in your browser. We use cartoon avatars instead of real photos so your identity stays completely anonymous.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Our tool is for entertainment and educational purposes only. It is NOT a substitute for professional therapy, counseling, or medical advice. If you are experiencing serious emotional distress, please reach out to a licensed therapist or mental health professional.
